# Industrial L4 direction-local literal-scale review rubric

This is a binary recommendation rubric with no points or score. It is applied
unchanged to one exact direction-local v2 `source_candidate` packet at a time.
Every visual gate must support pass for QA to recommend pass to Integration.

## 1. Exact actual-pixel identity

Literal color, literal grayscale, native-2x, three LODs,
registration/contact, silhouette, and accepted-L3 comparison surfaces must be
untouched committed bytes named by the exact packet review manifest. Color and
grayscale must depict the same source identity. Any missing, stale, generated
after review, annotated, rescaled, substituted, or hash-drifting surface
supports return or blocks review.

## 2. Two-second player recognition

At untouched `192 x 128`, color and grayscale must independently read within
two seconds as one premium high-tier heavy-industrial works. The read may not
depend on labels, pseudo-text, tiny decorative detail, author explanation, or
zoom. Residential, Commercial, civic, campus, airport, generic warehouse,
generic utility, or disconnected-prop ambiguity supports return.

## 3. Hierarchy, silhouette, and L4 progression

The building must have one coherent production hierarchy: a dominant
industrial working mass, a legible production/freight story, and subordinate
supporting elements. The silhouette must be deliberate rather than noisy,
fragmented, toy-like, or a collection of unrelated props. At the same scale,
it must be visibly more advanced and crafted than accepted Industrial L3
without becoming sterile, imported, or disconnected from CitySim.

## 4. Runtime frontage truth

The authored face must communicate a believable road-served logistics
connection at the declared runtime direction and exact source-pixel socket.
Freight and human-scale access must not contradict each other or rely on a
mask-only semantic overlay. Wrong-edge entrance, ambiguous road connection,
camera-facing substitution, DCC/world-label reasoning, mirror, rotation, or
per-direction transform supports return.

## 5. Material and grayscale value structure

Color must show a restrained, coherent Industrial material hierarchy.
Grayscale must preserve the dominant mass, entrance/logistics read, primary
production feature, ground contact, and supporting elements. Chalky or
clinical whites, neon/chroma noise, muddy dark collapse, low-contrast
flatness, excessive grain, outline-only form, or detail that disappears in
grayscale supports return.

## 6. Grounding, registration, and raster integrity

The building must sit on its footprint with coherent contact shadow and no
float, paste, detachment, collision-looking overlap, crop, socket drift,
frontage drift, alpha fringe, chroma halo, hidden-RGB artifact, or shadow
separation. Registration/contact overlays diagnose truth but do not replace a
player-visible grounded read.

## 7. Compact and LOD survival

City preserves district-scale heavy-industry silhouette. Neighborhood
preserves primary hierarchy and road-facing logistics. Block preserves
material craft and entrance meaning. Literal grayscale must survive alongside
color. Blank, sparse, aliased, fallback, recolored, or materially different
LOD output supports return.

## 8. Direction-local family discipline

The packet must remain consistent with its exact shared appearance/material
authorities and accepted L3 world language, while being independently authored
for its direction. It may not clone, mirror, rotate, recolor, or fall back to
another direction or accepted master. Cross-direction production cohesion is
explicitly deferred to the one exact 4/4 renderer-candidate staged-app gate.

## Automatic block or return

- `BLOCK_INPUT_IDENTITY`: packet/receipt/path/hash/commit/schema/direction or
  actual-pixel identity is missing, stale, mutable, or ambiguous.
- `QA_RECOMMEND_RETURN_TO_DIRECTION_CELL`: any visual gate above fails at
  untouched actual scale.
- Author narration, sibling coaching, metadata metrics, or technical green
  gates may not convert a visual failure into pass.
- A direction-local pass recommendation never means source admission,
  renderer ingestion, production selection, or app acceptance.
