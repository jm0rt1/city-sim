# Wave 003 — Make It Feel Like a Game

**Status:** Dispatched from accepted Wave 002 publication `74b694d` on July 20, 2026

**Date:** July 20, 2026

## Product promise

The next accepted build must move beyond a technically complete city editor into a paced strategy game. A 20-minute session should contain a clear opening choice, a readable city response, a strategy-specific complication, a recovery decision, and an unmistakable result. The city world and command center must explain that arc without developer coaching.

## Exit measures

- First meaningful decision within two minutes.
- No unexplained dead-time interval longer than 30 seconds.
- A material decision produces readable numerical and world feedback within 15 seconds of relevant simulation time.
- At least three consequential decision moments occur before the final outcome.
- Recovery is possible before minute 18, but avoiding the setback entirely requires a real tradeoff.
- Commercial and industrial approaches remain viable while producing visibly and mechanically different cities.
- Pointer, keyboard-only, Full Keyboard Access, VoiceOver, compact, save/resume, undo, and corruption-recovery journeys pass without coaching.

## Queued lane outcomes

### Gameplay — PLAY-012: Deliver a three-act playable session

Create an opening fork, strategy-specific midgame complication, recovery decision, and clear success/failure payoff. Compress passive waiting and ensure another meaningful choice arrives every few minutes. Preserve determinism and save compatibility.

### Simulation platform — PLAY-041: Publish spatial consequence truth

Define the smallest deterministic presentation contract for per-location utility service, pollution, prosperity/strain, recovery, and stable event identity. Renderer and UI consume it; they do not infer or duplicate it. Include replay, save/load, undo, fingerprint, and performance consequences.

### World rendering — PLAY-022: Make strategy reshape the living city

Build on accepted PLAY-021 art. Show commercial and industrial identity, density progression, utility trouble, pollution, prosperity, decline, and recovery through authored architecture, environment, effects, and bounded ambient life. The live staged city—not a fixture—must visibly evolve across the three-act session.

### UI and input — PLAY-032: Turn diagnosis into direct action

Every important warning and selected problem exposes cause, consequence, and legitimate remedies. Add governed keyboard world navigation so spatial play is no longer the command-system exception. Preserve map dominance and compact usability.

### Playtest quality — PLAY-051: Prove fun, comprehension, and replay value

Run the complete 20-minute pointer and keyboard journeys. Record decision cadence, dead time, consequence latency, confusion, false feedback, recovery, dominant strategy, visual storytelling, accessibility, save/resume trust, and whether the session creates a clear desire to replay differently.
