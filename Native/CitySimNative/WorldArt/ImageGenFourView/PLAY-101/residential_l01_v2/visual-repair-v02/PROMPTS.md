# PLAY-097 Residential L1 v2 visual-repair prompts

All four assets were produced with the built-in ImageGen tool at 1536×1024 on a uniform `#ff00ff` key. Each call required true 2:1 orthographic isometric projection, the CitySim footprint/pivot/socket contract, northwest key light, a contained southeast shadow, and no road, curb, public sidewalk, extra structure, car, person, text, UI, mirror, rotation, alias, copied facade, recolor-only substitution, or rejected-pixel reuse.

## North v02 — identity freeze

Create a complete lived-in low-density lot around a modest one-and-a-half-story American Craftsman cottage: broad medium-value warm-gray hipped roof with a shallow dormer, cream/taupe clapboard, deep-green trim, red-brick chimney and foundation accents, legible covered porch and entry, coherent windows, a diamond lawn, short private walk, mailbox, modest foundation shrubs and flower beds, and restrained porch furnishings. Reject icon/kiosk scale, empty pads, civic massing, black roofs, or decorative clutter.

## East v02

Using North only as the frozen identity reference, independently author the East road-facing side with a shorter side porch, legible side entry, paired dining-room windows, distinct planting rhythm, and a short East-socket walk while preserving the household architecture, palette, scale, roof, and yard vocabulary.

## South v02

Using North only as the frozen identity reference, independently author the opposite South side with a broad rear porch/sunroom transition, legible household entry, glazed doors, small kitchen garden, service planting, and a short South-socket walk while preserving the same family identity and scale.

## West v02

Using North only as the frozen identity reference, independently author the West side with an asymmetrical covered side porch, secondary entry, projecting bay window, one small bicycle occupancy cue, restrained planting, and a short West-socket walk while preserving the same family identity and scale.
