# PLAY-068 Frozen 20-Point Wave 008 Rubric

This rubric was frozen before receipt or inspection of a PLAY-064/065/066/067
candidate. Each category is scored from zero through four. Every lost point
must name an observable cause, exact route, and candidate-bound evidence.

## 1. Consequential post-Charter second act — minimum 3/4

Four requires:

- the Charter to open, rather than end, consequential play;
- typed, visible `warning -> setback -> recovery -> qualification -> payoff`
  phases for both Commercial and Industrial strategies;
- materially different warned pressure, recovery decision, standards, and
  payoff for the two strategies;
- at least one real recoverable failure, concrete objective/message remedy,
  and no strategy that is trivially dominant;
- qualification only at authoritative daily boundaries, consecutive-day
  progress, reset on failed standards, and no transient-spike credit;
- one-time Regional Capital recognition only after all strategy-specific
  standards hold for the exact contract interval; and
- exact undo, save/load, backup recovery, replay, fingerprint, and legacy
  decode behavior.

Three permits one non-blocking tuning limitation that does not make the second
act cosmetic, confusing, terminal, trivial, or unrecoverable.

## 2. World, public-realm, and activity truth — required 4/4

Four requires materially richer and more coherent parks, streets, sidewalks,
curbs, vegetation, parking/service yards, fences, lot edges, furniture,
lamps/signage, and restrained ambient life at city, neighborhood, and block
LOD in both widths.

Every prop, actor, shadow, socket, pivot, footprint, exclusion zone, and LOD
must be deterministic, grounded, non-overlapping, and consistent with the
accepted world language. Activity density must come only from accepted
coordinate-scoped `streetActivityIndex` and `placeActivityIndex` values:

- nil and zero suppress;
- low/high/recovery observations are monotonic and repeatable;
- the presentation may say only local activity or activity pressure;
- no actor, label, tooltip, AX value, overlay, or animation may imply a person
  count, trip, route, service, prosperity, pollution, or gameplay fact absent
  from the snapshot; and
- Reduce Motion preserves the same static meaning without hidden state.

Any invented truth, overlap, fallback, nondeterminism, or compact richness
loss makes this category less than four and automatically rejects.

## 3. Game/HUD cohesion and map aperture — required 4/4

Four requires a modern situational command layer that preserves:

- current objective, trajectory, urgency, treasury/economy, notices, speed,
  selected-target identity/state, and the most relevant action at a glance;
- the identical active coordinate, availability, reason, and command across
  normal, Details, Focus City, overlays, selection, construction, and updates;
- progressive Details that remains map-preserving rather than becoming a wall
  of panels;
- stable typography, hierarchy, contrast, clipping, scrolling, and focus at
  regular and exact 900 x 600; and
- a material, measurable composition preference over the frozen baseline.

Using the frozen vertical-band method, normal and Details must each improve
usable map aperture by at least five percentage points at both widths; Focus
City may not regress by more than two points. A different surface shape may
claim equivalent improvement only when retained pixel masks prove at least
the same unobscured world area. Urgency, active target, action, or compact
truth may not be sacrificed to gain aperture.

## 4. Playability, control, persistence, and accessibility — minimum 3/4

Four requires pointer, Return, Space, menu, command guide, FKA, and AX to
announce and mutate exactly one identical target once. It also requires:

- truthful valid/invalid preview and durable exact rejection reason;
- no pointer leak, double activation, stale target, focus-generation drift,
  text-field shortcut leakage, or modal leakage;
- command-guide search and application menus to disclose the same availability
  and disabled reason as the map/HUD;
- topmost-first Escape with stable focus restoration;
- normal/Focus/Details/overlay/selection/construction state continuity;
- exact undo fingerprint and save/terminate/relaunch/load-paused plus
  backup-only recovery; and
- visual, FKA, VoiceOver/AX, and Reduce Motion parity for all critical actions.

Three permits one disclosed, non-blocking limitation with no false feedback,
inaccessible action, persistence mismatch, or required coaching.

## 5. Shipping identity, determinism, and performance — minimum 3/4

Four requires exact source tree, bundle, executable, manifest, resource,
fixture, digest, defaults, root, PID, window, camera, and capture identity;
source/staged parity; deterministic two-build output; zero fallbacks; clean
pack/geometry/collision validators; focused and full test passes; and governed
staged verification.

Binding budgets:

- at most four active 2048 x 2048 pages;
- approximately 96 MiB target active texture memory and 128 MiB hard repeated
  LOD high-water;
- compressed shipping pack at or below 128 MiB;
- staged settled RSS no more than accepted baseline plus 128 MiB and no
  continuing high-water growth;
- unchanged-pulse average at or below 2.1 ms;
- no existing cold/update/render metric regression over 20 percent without
  integration-approved authority;
- LOD transition p95 below 16.7 ms and maximum below 33.3 ms; and
- no node, drawable, action, page, decoded-byte, texture, or RSS accumulation.

Three permits one explained non-player-blocking limitation that does not
weaken identity, determinism, fallback, hard budgets, or the mandatory
categories.
