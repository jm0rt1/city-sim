Date: 2026-07-25

Status: PLAN FROZEN; SOURCE EXPANSION AND RENDERER INGESTION NOT AUTHORIZED

## Outcome

The next smallest high-impact world-art batch is one complete directional
Industrial L2 variant-zero family:

- `industrial_l02_north_v0`
- `industrial_l02_east_v0`
- `industrial_l02_south_v0`
- `industrial_l02_west_v0`

The independently approved East v05 review candidate at
`023407791bbebe81f882dd8c7a4b348b79c22e67` is the immutable family anchor. It
is not yet source authority and remains `productionSelected: false`. North,
South, and West must be separately authored. They may not be rotations,
mirrors, aliases, fallbacks, or transformed siblings.

This batch is deliberately smaller than an Industrial L2-L4 program and larger
than another one-direction proof. Four directions are the minimum shippable
unit because the active renderer selects authored frontage by road direction.

## Expected visible result

At an upgraded Industrial lot, the player should immediately see a credible
medium logistics/manufacturing campus rather than Industrial L1 or a missing
level identity:

- a broad, low production hall;
- a distinct administration and quality-control wing;
- three grounded loading bays with readable canopies and aprons;
- a staff entrance that cannot be mistaken for a fourth dock;
- subordinate safety-orange accents;
- visible roof plant and duct hierarchy;
- a materially richer color and grayscale read than Industrial L1 at block,
  neighborhood, and city LODs.

The batch improves one complete player-facing density step without waiting for
Industrial L3/L4. The default-opening proof should be scheduled with the
PLAY-076 three-block starter-town expansion so the new industrial density is
actually present in the staged journey. An upgraded-state proof can be captured
earlier from an exact save/fixture once the renderer-owned ingestion is
authorized.

## Frozen short cadence

### Checkpoint A — authority and pre-pixel freeze

Integration publishes an updated PLAY-027 claim that:

1. promotes or explicitly carries East v05 into the four-view candidate;
2. authorizes North, South, and West source authoring;
3. leaves Industrial L3/L4 blocked;
4. names the exact synchronized baseline.

World Art then freezes three new direction-specific descriptors and proves:

- unique descriptor and geometry identities;
- exact footprint, pivot, socket, door-base, light, and authored-shadow
  registration;
- no sibling transforms or cross-family aliases;
- actual-scale analytic color and grayscale panels beside accepted Industrial
  L1 and the East v05 anchor;
- large-form and feature-size budgets that keep all identity-bearing elements
  readable at native-2x.

### Checkpoint B — one governed primary per new direction

Render exactly one fresh Metal-visible North, South, and West primary from the
frozen descriptors. East v05 remains byte-exact.

Stop at the first visual or technical failure. The four-view primary contact
must prove complete grounded silhouettes, correct road-facing loading
infrastructure, consistent family materials, and materially distinct authored
directions before repeat work starts.

### Checkpoint C — deterministic source candidate

Only after primary review:

- run B and C in fresh processes for each new direction;
- require exact emitted-PNG and decoded-RGBA identity per direction;
- normalize every direction exactly twice to block, neighborhood, and city;
- require byte and decoded-pixel identity across normalization runs;
- require four unique source identities and twelve unique normalized
  identities;
- run alpha, chroma, visible-spill, hidden-RGB, padding, occupancy, pivot,
  socket, frontage, contact-shadow, and projection checks;
- require an empty hash intersection with every accepted Residential,
  Commercial, and Industrial L1 source and LOD;
- publish source-scale, native-2x, registered-footprint, zoom, block,
  neighborhood, and city color and grayscale panels;
- retain every attempt, prompt/material provenance, rejection, toolchain hash,
  and `productionSelected: false`.

Independent art review freezes one clean four-direction source candidate. World
Art does not ingest it into the product.

### Checkpoint D — renderer-owned staged-app handoff

A new renderer-owned claim, sequenced without mutating active PLAY-073 work,
may ingest only the exact independently accepted four-direction candidate.
That handoff must not be performed on the World Art branch.

The renderer checkpoint must prove:

- no runtime mirror, rotation, alias, or fallback;
- deterministic two-build atlas/page/manifest identity;
- source-to-packed-pixel identity and four-direction coverage;
- stable LOD thresholds, pivot, socket, footprint, shadow, and road frontage;
- no regression to accepted Residential, Commercial, or Industrial L1 art;
- focused world-rendering tests, full native tests, and
  `./script/build_and_run.sh --verify`;
- clean task-owned candidate with no Package.swift, shared build, gameplay,
  simulation, save, or UI mutation.
