## Transient-clear procedure

For every binding route:

1. launch one exact isolated PID;
2. load the byte-identical quicksave with Command-O;
3. wait at least 5.5 seconds after load/action feedback disappears;
4. select Industrial block 15,12 with four Right presses;
5. confirm Day 33, paused state, selected coordinate, overlay, HUD mode, and
   AX target;
6. wait another 5.5 seconds after opening Details or Focus when a transient is
   generated;
7. capture the uncropped decorated window and full AX tree; and
8. bind PID/environment/RSS, then terminate only that PID.

No binding frame contains `Action update`, `Action cancelled`, load feedback,
or another expiring surface. The persistent `Utilities` priority action is
intentionally retained.
