## Matrix maintenance rules

- A row becomes `Mapped` only after its complete acceptance criteria pass and retained evidence is linked.
- Partial prototype evidence remains labeled as partial until production architecture and release thresholds are satisfied.
- Closing a design decision updates every dependent design-gap row in the same change.
- A requirement cannot be removed from this table; it is marked `Superseded` and linked to its replacement.
- The matrix validator runs in continuous integration and at every production gate.
