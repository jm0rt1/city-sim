## Why the process resets

The prior analytic scaffold encouraged technically valid variations of one
compact-box grammar. More engineering on that scaffold will not produce a
beautiful landmark. World Art must establish a compelling visual target first,
then translate the accepted target into deterministic N/E/S/W geometry in a
later authority.

Whole-building image generation is authorized in this phase only as
non-authoritative concept art. It is not directional source art, cannot enter
the normalizer or renderer, and cannot establish footprint, pivot, frontage,
lighting, shadow, or production authority.

## Required concept set

Use the built-in ImageGen workflow in separate calls to create at least four
high-quality isometric concept images:

1. **Turbine Works** — a wide, low advanced foundry with a long high-bay hall,
   sawtooth or monitor roof, rear furnace/stack, overhead crane gantry, and
   warm masonry control wing.
2. **Copperline Fabrication Campus** — an asymmetric assembly hall, broad
   freight court, elevated pipe bridge, silos, dark service recesses, and a
   small human-scale glazed control entrance.
3. **Atlas Robotics Forge** — a modern heavy-manufacturing complex with broad
   skylit production bays, a restrained control tower, visible roof plant,
   large logistics doors, and credible industrial aging.
4. **Riversteel Works** — a layered steel-and-brick fabrication plant with a
   stepped roofline, paired stacks, external ducts, gantry crane, and a strong
   horizontal silhouette.

Every concept prompt must request:

- premium high-fidelity isometric city-building game art;
- a clearly wider-than-tall, grounded campus silhouette;
- readable large-scale operations before small detail;
- weathered brick or warm formed concrete, dark blue-green/charcoal steel,
  oxide/copper/ochre accents, deep service shadows, believable glazing, roof
  plant, joints, stains, and material aging;
- two or three large freight openings plus a separate staff/control entrance;
- northwest light and southeast contact shadow;
- neutral isolated ground, no roads, no city background, no labels, no UI,
  and no text;
- no white/cyan/cream dominant plane, office tower, apartment, retail
  storefront, glossy sci-fi factory, miniature toy, or generic cube stack.

The four concepts must not be prompt synonyms. Vary overall organization,
roofline, skyline feature, logistics layout, and relationship between the
production hall and control wing.

## Evidence and stop

Preserve each exact prompt, ImageGen artifact identifier, output bytes/hash,
timestamp, and rejection notes. Build:

- one equal-scale four-concept contact sheet;
- one grayscale silhouette sheet;
- one compact gameplay-size sheet;
- one comparison against rejected v01 and returned analytic v02; and
- a concise art-director scorecard rating landmark silhouette, functional
  readability, material richness, CitySim cohesion, compact recognition,
  non-commercial identity, and feasibility for separately authored N/E/S/W.

Do not select a winner automatically. Commit one clean concept-art packet with
`sourceAuthority=false` and `productionSelected=false`, then stop for
independent integration review. No descriptors, material libraries, toolchain,
analytic geometry, source renders, normalization, renderer/shipping files,
package/runtime files, push, integration, or self-acceptance.
