## Player outcome

The authentic opening and every governed later state must read as one authored,
inhabited district rather than a small strip of buildings and roads placed on
a broad green board. Buildings, public realm, terrain, camera, interaction, and
the responsive HUD must form one coherent presentation at regular and exact
900 x 600 layouts.

This closeout begins only after the accepted Industrial source batches have
published or been explicitly deferred. Industrial L3/L4 improve late-game
Industrial progression; they do not satisfy this contract by themselves.

## Automatic rejects

Reject R4 if any of the following remains:

1. The perceptual district/public-realm envelope occupies less than 60% of safe
   map width at either regular or exact 900 x 600 layout.
2. Any plain-terrain component exceeds 25% of the safe aperture, or plain grass
   remains the largest authored visual mass.
3. Camera improvement is achieved by clipping every complete buildable parcel
   band outside the developed edge.
4. A road mask, junction, curb, sidewalk, crossing, frontage, or entrance seam
   is broken at any governed LOD.
5. A building, park, utility, service yard, shadow, base, or parcel plate reads
   as floating, pasted on, clipped, or detached in native-scale review.
6. Adjacent places reuse the same source-and-context signature when accepted
   alternatives exist.
7. A building is mirrored, rotated, recolored, aliased, substituted across
   families, or silently replaced by fallback art.
8. City, neighborhood, and block LOD frames are resized equivalents rather
   than materially different presentations of district, frontage, and entrance
   truth.
9. Selection, preview, overlays, or compact Details obscure the target,
   nearest-road relationship, or surrounding district context.
10. The renderer exceeds the accepted resource and timing ceilings without a
    separately approved budget change.

## Ownership and implementation order

World Rendering owns camera composition, terrain/public-realm composition,
road visual weight, parcel and special-lot grounding, deterministic context,
LOD presentation, selection framing, renderer reuse, and diagnostics.

World Art owns separately authorized source families and may not repair
shipping composition. Additional park, utility/service, Residential, or
Commercial source breadth requires a separate published source claim; the
renderer may not fake that breadth through transforms or aliases.

The order is:

1. publish or explicitly defer Industrial L3 through R2;
2. publish or explicitly defer Industrial L4 through R3;
3. synchronize the clean renderer lane to the resulting baseline;
4. implement the systemic R4 composition and cohesion slice;
5. run the complete integration gate and exact staged journeys; and
6. submit the exact combined candidate to the full independent PLAY-075 20/20
   release gate.

## Completion boundary

Automated geometry, collision, hash, pack, and performance results are
necessary but insufficient. R4 is complete only when the exact staged app is
materially preferred to the R1 baseline at regular and compact sizes, every
automatic reject is absent, the complete PLAY-075 rubric scores 20/20 with
every category 4/4, and integration publishes the clean accepted baseline.
