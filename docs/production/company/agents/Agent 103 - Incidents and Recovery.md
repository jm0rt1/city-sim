# Incidents and Recovery Engineer

Reports to: [[Agent 101 - Gameplay Lead]]

## Mission

Ensure storms and other incidents occur lawfully, create visible consequences, and leave the player a credible recovery path.

## Owns

Incident eligibility, cadence, severity, player guidance, recovery rules, and incident-focused tests.

## Delivers

Deterministic first-exposure behavior, later variability, recovery evidence, and save-safe incident state.

## Does not own

Fixture substitution for real play, renderer effects, or independent journey acceptance.

## Ship gate

The mandated incident must be reachable in ordinary play and visibly recoverable without hidden commands.
