# Industrial L4 North v05 camera-visibility addendum

**Superseded world-space premise:** Canonical descriptors and the renderer
establish North as `z = -28`, not `x = -28`; the v06 coordinate-bridge
authority records and governs the repair. This addendum remains historical
rejection evidence and is no longer operative authority.

**Parent authority:** `INDUSTRIAL-L04-BLENDER-NORTH-ART-V05-AUTHORITY.md`

**Historical disposition:** `BINDING_PRE_RENDER_GATE`

The frozen camera is on the `+X/+Z` side while the corrected road-facing portal
must face outward from the `X = -28` footprint side. The socket-facing facade
is therefore camera-far. Plane/socket agreement alone does not prove that the
player can see or understand the entrance.

Before process A, the zero-pixel actual-camera proof must show:

- portal outward normal `-X`;
- the complete void, both jambs, header, reveals, threshold, and exterior
  apron as nonzero contiguous projected regions with zero hall/process
  occlusion;
- the threshold inside the footprint, leaving a legal exterior court between
  it and the socket;
- one final-camera visible apron component touching the threshold and reaching
  within two compact pixels of the socket, never hidden beneath mass;
- compact portal envelope at least `15 × 13`, frame at least two pixels, and a
  complete staff/equivalent scale cue at least `4 × 8`;
- no occupied-bounds or silhouette shrink from v03, whose source occupied
  bounds were `501 × 403`;
- all four monitor caps, three height breaks, broad hall, process bay, stack,
  registration, and southeast shadow preserved.

If any item fails, World Art commits the zero-pixel return and does not render
A. If they pass, the one-A cadence from the parent authority remains in force.

Independent A review must reject the candidate unless unaided original-size
192×128 color and grayscale views make the open freight portal, narrowing
roadward apron, separate human-scale cue, and stronger-than-L3 tier progression
immediately legible. Overlays may corroborate but cannot substitute for literal
visibility.
