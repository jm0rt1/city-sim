# Wave 011 — World Art Cohesion and Breadth Program

**Owner:** World Art through PLAY-027

**Shipping owner:** World Rendering through separately reserved ingestion
windows

**Priority:** Replace the current mixed-fidelity catalog with a richer,
cohesive, native-scale art system that makes the authentic city attractive,
recognizable, and varied during actual play—not only in source-scale sheets.

## Why this program exists

The accepted R1 and current R2 staged frames prove that adding one technically
correct family at a time is not sufficient. Industrial L3 is readable, but its
bright clean materials sit beside dark Residential roofs, brick factories,
ornate civic art, sparse parks, and utility props from visibly different value,
outline, material, and detail systems. Repeated opening Residential identities
also remain obvious.

The art lane must now optimize for the composed game frame:

- coherent northwest light, southeast shadow, outline weight, saturation,
  contrast, material grain, and ground contact across every family;
- silhouettes and entrances that remain readable at native gameplay scale;
- enough independently authored variants to stop adjacent copy reads;
- materially richer surfaces without fragile subpixel noise;
- real N/E/S/W frontage views, never runtime mirroring or rotation; and
- stable pivot, footprint, socket, shadow, padding, and deterministic
  normalization compatible with the existing shipping pipeline.

## Production order

### A1 — Industrial L4 hero family

Start immediately in parallel with the R2 Industrial L3 renderer window.
Author one separately modeled N/E/S/W Industrial L4 family that completes the
variant-zero R/C/I spine and establishes the stronger art bar:

- unmistakable advanced-industrial silhouette and capability progression;
- richer, less sterile materials than L3 while remaining in the shared value
  system;
- a legible administration/frontage volume, high-bay/process mass, rooftop
  plant, tanks/stacks, loading or service language, and believable ground
  contact;
- color and grayscale recognition at source, native-2x, registered footprint,
  neighborhood, and city scales; and
- explicit comparison against Industrial L1/L2/L3 plus Residential and
  Commercial L4.

R3 ingestion remains gated on R2 publication and independent source review.
Source work may run ahead; shipping work may not.

### A2 — Opening diversity kit

After A1 reaches one independently reviewable clean candidate, author a
controlled opening-visible breadth batch:

- Residential L1 variants 1 and 2, N/E/S/W;
- Commercial L1 variant 1, N/E/S/W; and
- Industrial L1 variant 1, N/E/S/W.

Every variant must be a separately authored building identity, not a recolor,
prop swap, crop, transform, or facade-only alias. The packet must prove
unlabeled color/grayscale family recognition, non-intersection with every
accepted source and normalized identity, and side-by-side opening-scale
repetition reduction. Renderer selection and shipping require a later
integration-owned deterministic-variant contract and reserved ingestion slot.

### A3 — Special-place cohesion kit

Audit and then author source replacements for the highest-impact mixed-fidelity
special places visible in the authentic opening and mature city: park/plaza,
civic, utility, and service-yard families. Freeze exact logical identities,
footprints, orientation needs, interaction boundaries, and renderer ownership
before pixels. Split this into small independently reviewable batches; do not
create one unreviewable atlas dump.

## Binding quality bar

Reject a source batch if any of the following is true:

1. It looks attractive only at source scale but loses silhouette, frontage, or
   material hierarchy at native gameplay scale.
2. Its brightness, saturation, outline, shadow, or ground plate reads as a
   different game beside the accepted catalog.
3. A direction is mirrored, rotated, or compositionally inconsistent.
4. Two logical identities alias, or a variant is only a recolor/prop swap.
5. Corrugation, windows, railings, roof equipment, or texture becomes unstable
   subpixel noise after normalization.
6. The building floats, clips its contact shadow, obscures its frontage, or
   exceeds its governed footprint/padding.
7. The review packet omits regular-scale and compact-scale composed comparison
   frames against the current staged baseline.
8. The lane edits renderer, shipping atlas, production selection, package,
   gameplay, simulation, UI, save, or shared-manifest surfaces.

## Fast source cadence

1. Freeze one pre-pixel architecture and material/value target.
2. Render one direction and review source plus native-scale survival before
   authoring siblings.
3. Complete N/E/S/W as one family; retain every attempt and rejection.
4. Normalize twice from each accepted immutable master.
5. Produce exact color/grayscale, footprint, zoom, LOD, progression,
   cross-family, and staged-baseline comparison sheets.
6. Commit one clean candidate and stop for independent integration review.
7. Let World Rendering ingest only through its reserved single-writer window.

Whole-building ImageGen remains closed by the retained PLAY-027 capability
evidence. Image generation may support non-compositional material swatches or
reference exploration only when provenance is retained and the governed
offline scene remains the geometry, camera, frontage, light, shadow, and
registration authority.
