# Single-angle ImageGen production

Read this reference for every CONTRACT-024 asset, batch, normalization, contact
sheet, handoff, or repair.

## Cell model

One cell owns one disjoint family or shared-toolchain claim. A family cell may
generate separate asset identities concurrently in isolated tool outputs, but
one visible owner alone adopts files, stages, validates, and commits the claim.
Do not edit another family, the shared toolchain, renderer, shipping pack, or
Integration authority.

## Generation

Use `$imagegen` in built-in mode. Make one call per logical asset identity. Use
the exact style and registration references bound by CONTRACT-024. Prompt one
centered orthographic 2:1 building on a perfectly flat solid `#ff00ff` field.
Require generous padding, contained southeast shadow, no road, district, extra
building, text, UI, logo, watermark, crop, perspective convergence, toy/voxel
look, or frame-edge subject pixels.

Store the final project-bound raw under the claimed source root immediately.
Retain the prompt, logical ID, tool result/artifact identifier when exposed,
date, reference paths and hashes, raw file hash, intended gameplay meaning, and
candidate disposition. Never silently use CLI/API fallback. A failed built-in
call or chroma path stops for Integration direction.

## Mechanical gates

The Integration-approved PLAY-096 harness alone defines normalization and
handoff shape. Until it is published, family cells may retain raw candidates
and provenance but may not invent a competing normalizer or claim source
admission.

For each identity require canonical RGBA, zero hidden RGB, no visible chroma
spill, transparent corners, adequate padding, exact pivot, non-clipped shadow,
three explicit LODs, prompt/provenance completeness, and unique raw plus
normalized decoded hashes. Generate color and grayscale contact sheets at
source, native-2x, and literal gameplay scale.

Static file presence does not prove image quality. Luna may report a candidate
and mechanical PASS only. Frontier review alone may accept appearance and
Integration alone may admit a source or activate shipping art.

## Repair and stop rules

Return only the failed identity. Do not regenerate passing sibling identities.
After two unsuccessful repairs of one identity, preserve all attempts and
escalate. Stop immediately on shared-contract need, path escape, alias,
unresolved visual judgment, reference drift, unsupported model route, or a
request to self-accept, integrate, push, or pin a task.
