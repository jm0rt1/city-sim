# PLAY-027 independent art rejection

**Disposition:** calibration rejected as art; technical evidence retained

Integration inspected the source-scale, native-2x, and unlabeled grayscale
contact sheets directly and rejected the residential L1 variant-zero set.

The exact defects are:

1. North and west show no readable entrance/frontage; the road-facing identity
   disappears behind the mass, violating frontage correctness and game-scale
   readability.
2. East and south entrance cues are too small and effectively vanish at
   native-2x.
3. All four views read as almost the same dark rectangular box in grayscale;
   hash-distinct directional authorship is not visually distinct authorship.
4. The residential L1 silhouette and materials are too austere and repetitive:
   flat dark walls, a repetitive window grid, an oversized plain roof, and
   minimal residential character.
5. The contact sheets allocate too much empty field to judge practical sprite
   readability efficiently.

The set remains `productionSelected: false`. No source in this set is accepted,
and the remaining 44 sources remain blocked. The replacement calibration must
make every frontage unmistakable at native-2x through direction-specific
massing and occlusion design, strengthen porch/stoop/roof/window/material
hierarchy and warm grayscale separation, and add exact-footprint plus zoomed
review panels without deriving any sibling by mirror, rotation, or transform.
