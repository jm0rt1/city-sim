## Player-facing target

Industrial L4 must become a city-defining advanced-manufacturing landmark,
not another interchangeable box or pale imported tower. At normal gameplay
scale it should immediately communicate major productive capacity, heavy
operations, skilled employment, and a credible road-facing working frontage.

The player should be able to distinguish it from Industrial L1–L3 and from
Residential or Commercial L4 without labels, in both color and grayscale.

## Rejected boundary

Preserve exact rejected checkpoint
`d5ba53412a5e993d677d8c25920d1e471df06191` and its evidence unchanged.
Version 01 remains rejected because it reads as:

- a compact generic tower rather than a broad manufacturing campus;
- sterile pale blue-white/cream material planes;
- skeletal North/West but solid East/South;
- weakly differentiated control, production, tank, roof-plant, and heavy
  service functions; and
- five storefront-like bays instead of credible industrial logistics.

Version 02 must be a fresh descriptor/material/tool/evidence namespace. It may
reuse frozen projection, pivot, socket, light, shadow, and deterministic
sampling contracts, but it may not inherit version 01 massing or palette by
default.

## Required design language

Develop three genuinely distinct pre-pixel concept candidates before freezing
one:

1. a broad turbine/foundry works with a strong sawtooth or monitor-roof hall;
2. an asymmetric precision-manufacturing campus with an elevated control wing
   and visible process spine; and
3. a heavy fabrication works organized around a gantry, pipe bridge, silos,
   and high-bay assembly volume.

The selected concept must combine the strongest readable ideas into one
coherent, separately authored N/E/S/W family. It needs:

- a broad, grounded primary hall occupying the footprint instead of a narrow
  tower stack;
- one unmistakable high-bay production volume and one lower assembly/control
  volume;
- an asymmetric skyline feature visible at city LOD, such as a monitor roof,
  gantry, paired stack, process tower, or elevated bridge;
- two or three large credible freight/service openings, not a repeated
  storefront rhythm;
- a separate human-scale staff/control entrance on the authoritative frontage;
- legible roof plant, tanks/silos, ducts or pipework, and heavy-operation
  details without noisy micro-greebling;
- warm weathered masonry or formed concrete, dark painted steel, restrained
  oxide/ochre/copper safety accents, deep service recesses, and believable
  glass/roof values; and
- visible material aging, joints, depth, and contact grounding while remaining
  compatible with the accepted Industrial L3 cohesion family.

No dominant white, cyan, ice-blue, or cream planes. No compact office-tower
silhouette. No facade that could be mistaken for retail, apartments, or a
commercial high-rise.

Image generation may be used only for non-compositional material, weathering,
roof, masonry, metal, glass, or signage reference swatches. Preserve prompt,
tool artifact, provenance, and rejection records. Do not use generated
whole-building composition as directional source authority.

## Phase-one proof

Return one clean pre-pixel candidate containing:

1. a three-concept silhouette/material comparison with a written selection
   rationale;
2. independently authored N/E/S/W descriptors for the selected version 02
   family with unique descriptor and geometry identities;
3. exact pivot, footprint, frontage socket, door-base, contact, light, shadow,
   camera, and sampling validation;
4. zero camera-visible coincident structural/material-owner planes;
5. color, grayscale, and clay panels at source, native-2x, neighborhood, city,
   and compact gameplay scales;
6. Industrial L1–L4 progression panels and R/C/I L4 unlabeled comparisons;
7. a direct rejected-v01 versus v02 comparison at identical scale and neutral
   ground; and
8. a binding independent-review request that explicitly judges silhouette,
   palette, functional readability, directional parity, family cohesion, and
   gameplay-scale recognition.

The candidate must remain `sourceAuthority=false` and
`productionSelected=false`. Do not run SceneKit/Metal source renders or the
normalizer in this phase. Do not begin a raw gate until integration and
independent review accept the exact pre-pixel candidate.
