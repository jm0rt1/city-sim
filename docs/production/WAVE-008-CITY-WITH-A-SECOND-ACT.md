# Wave 008 — Build a City With a Second Act

## Why this wave exists

The published `1b883ca684b07ba38c5c755b616723bde0cd2230` baseline is the
first CitySim build whose Residential, Commercial, and Industrial L1
architecture is cohesive, road-facing, independently playable, and
production-selected. PLAY-063 approved the exact combined product at 20/20.

The remaining gap is no longer basic correctness. The city still reads as a
small board wrapped by a capable HUD, and the Town Charter behaves too much
like an ending. A strong next round must improve the game and the visible
world together:

1. the Charter must open a consequential second act rather than exhaust the
   progression;
2. streets, lots, parks, and public space must carry the same authored care as
   the buildings;
3. ambient life must be driven by authoritative local activity instead of
   renderer invention;
4. the HUD must feel like a modern situational command layer without covering
   the city; and
5. the combined result must survive an independent, candidate-blind real-app
   gate.

## Work order

### PLAY-064 — Make the Charter the midpoint

Gameplay owns a durable post-Charter chapter ending in Regional Capital
recognition. The committed Commercial or Industrial strategy must create a
different warned pressure, recovery path, and payoff. The chapter must be
recoverable, readable through existing objectives/messages, and reachable in
a no-coaching 20-minute session without trivializing the opening.

CONTRACT-015 authorizes the narrow optional progression extension. It does not
authorize a new command system, view architecture, or save schema.

### PLAY-065 — Give the world authoritative activity

Simulation platform adds deterministic, transient local street and place
activity indices to the existing immutable spatial consequence snapshot.
These values may describe presentation pressure only; they cannot change
economy, traffic, population, save bytes, fingerprints, or gameplay outcomes.

CONTRACT-016 defines the exact truth boundary. PLAY-066 may adopt the fields
only after the simulation candidate is accepted into the combined baseline.

### PLAY-066 — Finish the space between the buildings

World rendering delivers a street-scene and lot-context pass: authored civic
space, varied but coherent vegetation, sidewalks and curbs, parking/service
yards, fences and lot edges, street furniture, lamps/signage, and restrained
activity whose density comes only from accepted spatial truth.

The result must look materially richer at city, neighborhood, and block LOD
without overlap, visual noise, invented facts, fallback art, or unbounded
resource growth. This slice may use the accepted Industrial L1 product but
must not ingest pending Industrial L2–L4 art.

### PLAY-067 — Make the HUD breathe with the city

UI/input creates a more compelling situational command layer. The current
objective, city trajectory, urgency, selected-target truth, and most relevant
action must be legible at a glance, while the normal and exact 900 x 600
layouts expose materially more of the city. Details becomes a progressive,
map-preserving surface rather than a wall of panels.

Every action continues through the typed command catalog with pointer,
keyboard, command-guide, menu, focus, Escape, AX, FKA, and Reduce Motion
parity. This is presentation and composition work, not a parallel rules
engine.

### PLAY-068 — Independent second-act gate

Quality preregisters the exact `1b883ca` publication before receiving product
candidates. It observes a fresh staged session without coaching, compares the
same state and camera at regular and exact compact sizes, and scores the
combined product rather than accepting lane-authored evidence.

## Non-negotiable proof

- both Commercial and Industrial second-act routes, including warning,
  setback, recovery, recognition, undo, save/relaunch/load, and exact
  one-time outcome;
- a no-coaching fresh journey reaching meaningful post-Charter play inside
  20 minutes with a real choice and a recoverable failure;
- same-state city/neighborhood/block comparisons showing richer parks,
  streets, lot context, and public realm around accepted building art;
- deterministic identity and collision ledgers for every new prop, activity
  sprite, lot edge, shadow, socket, and LOD;
- retained regular and compact ambient observations with activity that rises,
  falls, and disappears only when the accepted snapshot says it should;
- normal, Focus City, and open-Details map-aperture measurements;
- pointer, keyboard, command guide, menu, Escape, focus restoration, AX, FKA,
  text-input quarantine, and Reduce Motion;
- exact staged resource identity, deterministic two-build pack parity, full
  native suite, renderer diagnostics, residency/RSS, and frame timing; and
- zero invented congestion, trips, population counts, services, prosperity,
  pollution, or hidden gameplay state.

## Acceptance bar

The exact combined candidate must score at least 19/20 with mandatory 4/4 in
world/public-realm coherence and game/HUD cohesion, no category below 3, zero
P0/P1 defects, and zero automatic rejects. It must be materially preferred to
`1b883ca` in regular and exact 900 x 600 same-state comparisons.

Automatic rejection includes a cosmetic-only second act, a milestone earned
from a transient spike, nondeterministic or overlapping props, ambient actors
that imply unavailable simulation truth, visual richness that disappears at
compact size, HUD changes that hide urgent state or move the active target,
keyboard/menu/AX drift, save or undo mismatch, fallback assets, unbounded
resource growth, or author-authored acceptance.
